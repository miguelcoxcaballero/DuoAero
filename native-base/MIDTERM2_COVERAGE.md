# Second-midterm coverage and release plan

The course-outline order is launch vehicles, space-flight environment,
space vehicles, the topic-4 introduction, diverse aircraft and rotorcraft.
The items below are a finite review checklist, not a claim that a section is
complete. The current development release-shaped path includes only the
first launch-vehicle node; no 2.0 release is authorized by this file.

| Block | Reviewed foundation | Remaining before full coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3.1 Launch vehicles | Ten introductory questions, ideal-rocket equation and circular-orbit sequences; native grading tested for the individual lessons and first-to-next navigation. | Complete and review orbital-energy relationships, rocket types, staging and recovery; exercise all three sessions in order and verify saved progress and restart. |
| 3.2 Space-flight environment | Base and extra concept lessons address free fall, vacuum, impacts, radiation, temperature, energy and life support. The revised extra lesson passed native grading and restart. | Complete gravity fields and perturbations, gravity-gradient effects, vacuum materials, impact mitigation, environmental radiation, thermal exchange, energy sources, life support and relativistic effects; rerun the revised base lesson. |

| 4 Topic-4 concepts | Ten introductory items cover system roles, control and propulsion; earlier wording passed native grading and restart. | The newly revised navigation/guidance/control and aerodynamic-force distinctions need native grading and source review; the rest of the outline remains uncovered. |

The new launch node uses `second-partial-3.1-lesson-0` through `-2` and its
existing stable level and skill IDs. No published first-midterm proof covers
these new exercises; no migration, completion, gems or XP is granted for them.
The release-shaped diagnostic checks those invariants before walking the path.
Production publication remains blocked on the complete course, original UI,
progress/reward behavior, animation and update acceptance described elsewhere.
